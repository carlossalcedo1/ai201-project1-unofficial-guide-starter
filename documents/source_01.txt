University of Florida Dining – Terms and Conditions 2025-2026

Meal Plan Swipe Policy
Students may enter The Eatery at Broward Hall or the Food Hall at Gator Corner once every 45 minutes per location. The 45-minute timer resets independently at each hall. Students may not transfer swipes to other individuals. Meal swipes are non-transferable and non-refundable.

Flex Dollar Policy
Flex dollars are a prepaid declining balance included with each UF meal plan. Unused flex dollars carry over from the fall semester to the spring semester of the same academic year. Any flex dollars remaining at the end of the spring semester are forfeited and will not roll over to the following academic year. Students are advised to monitor their flex balance through the Gator 1 Card online portal.

Guest Meals
Each student account is provisioned with ten guest meals per semester. Guest meals may be used to admit a visitor to any all-you-care-to-eat dining hall. Guest meals do not roll over between semesters.

Dining Hall Hours
The Eatery at Broward Hall: Monday–Friday 7:00am–10:00pm; Saturday–Sunday 9:00am–9:00pm.
The Food Hall at Gator Corner: Monday–Friday 7:00am–11:00pm; Saturday–Sunday 10:00am–10:00pm.
Hours are subject to change during university holidays, finals week, and summer sessions.

Meal Plan Options for 2025-2026
The Gold Plan includes 19 meals per week plus $200 flex dollars per semester.
The Orange Plan includes 14 meals per week plus $300 flex dollars per semester.
The Blue Plan includes 10 meals per week plus $400 flex dollars per semester.
The Gator Plan is a declining-balance-only plan with $1,200 flex dollars per semester and no swipe meals.
All plans include access to both all-you-care-to-eat dining halls.

Mandatory First-Year Requirement
All first-year students living in university residence halls are required to purchase one of the four standard meal plans. Students with documented dietary, medical, or religious requirements may petition for a plan exemption through the Office of Student Affairs.

Dietary Accommodation Process
Students with food allergies, celiac disease, or other medical dietary needs should contact Dining Services at least two weeks before the start of the semester. The campus registered dietitian will conduct a one-on-one consultation and develop an individualized meal accommodation plan. Common accommodations include dedicated allergen-free preparation areas, pre-portioned sealed meals, and advance notice of daily menus.

Cancellation Policy
Meal plans may not be cancelled after the first two weeks of the fall semester. Cancellations requested during the two-week grace period will receive a full refund of unused flex dollars and a prorated refund of unused meal swipes. No refunds are issued after the grace period except in cases of medical withdrawal or university-approved leave of absence.
