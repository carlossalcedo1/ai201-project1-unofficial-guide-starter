UF Vegan Dining Experience: An Honest Look – Florida Alligator, January 2024

When I signed up for the Orange Meal Plan as a first-year vegan transfer student, I had low expectations. UF's dining website lists "vegan options available daily" at both Broward and Gator Corner, but the website doesn't tell you that "available daily" sometimes means one dish that runs out by noon.

I visited both halls on a Tuesday in mid-January, arriving at Broward at 11:45am during the lunch rush. The vegan station — clearly marked with a green leaf icon above the sneeze guard — had three hot options: lentil soup, roasted chickpeas over saffron rice, and a tofu and vegetable stir-fry. I was pleasantly surprised. The tofu was properly seasoned and not watery, which is not something I expected from a dining hall.

I asked a dining staff member whether the tofu scramble I'd seen advertised was on the menu that day. She told me it rotates and appears two or three times per week, usually at breakfast. The campus dietitian, Dr. Maria Santos, was available for a brief comment. She confirmed that the tofu scramble and the lentil soup were both added to the rotation in fall 2023 after student feedback at the dining advisory board meeting.

Gator Corner told a different story. The vegan signage was less prominent — I had to ask a staff member to point me to the vegan options. There was one hot entrée marked vegan (black bean tacos), a salad bar with no clearly labeled vegan dressings, and hummus with pita bread. I checked the dressing bottles myself; only the balsamic vinaigrette was dairy-free.

Several vegan students I interviewed said they supplement dining hall meals with groceries. Freshman Maya Chen told me she buys tofu and produce from the Winn-Dixie on Archer Road once a week. "The dining halls are fine for variety if you're not vegan," she said, "but if you are, you learn where the gaps are pretty quickly."

My overall assessment: Broward is a 3/5 for vegans, Gator Corner is a 2/5. The options exist and the labeling is mostly accurate, but variety is limited and availability earlier in the day is better than at dinner.
