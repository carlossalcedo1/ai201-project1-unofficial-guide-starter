What to Know About Bite Club: A New Student Meal Plan Alternative – Florida Alligator, September 2024

A new meal plan alternative called Bite Club launched at UF in fall 2024, and it is already generating conversation among students who have long complained that UF's mandatory first-year meal plan options are too rigid and too expensive.

Bite Club operates as a declining-balance account loaded directly onto a student's Gator 1 Card. Unlike traditional swipe-based plans, Bite Club funds are accepted at participating off-campus restaurants in the Gainesville area in addition to on-campus dining locations. At launch, approximately 40 off-campus restaurants have signed on as partners, including several on University Avenue and SW 13th Street.

The impetus for Bite Club was years of student complaints documented in Florida Student Association surveys. The most common grievances: unused meal swipes at the end of each week (swipes do not roll over week-to-week under any current UF plan), the inability to use meal plan currency at off-campus restaurants, and the feeling of being locked into a high-cost plan with no flexibility.

Student Government Senator Priya Nair, who co-authored the resolution supporting the Bite Club pilot, said the goal was to give students more agency. "We wanted a plan that works for students who live off-campus by their second semester or who prefer local restaurants to the dining halls," she said.

There is a catch, however. Like standard flex dollars, Bite Club funds expire at the end of each semester. A student who loads $1,000 in August and has $200 remaining in December loses that $200. Critics, including a dissenting editorial in the Alligator, argued that Bite Club addresses the off-campus access problem but does not solve the underlying rollover issue.

The minimum Bite Club load is $250 per semester. Students can top up their balance at any time through the Gator 1 Card portal. The plan is available to all enrolled students, including first-year students who are also enrolled in a mandatory swipe plan, meaning some students may hold both plans simultaneously.
